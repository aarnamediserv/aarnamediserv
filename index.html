MediServ Compression Stocking Wearing Instruction

1
Choose the correct stocking as per the label mentioned on it for left or right leg.
2
Always use wearing aid which is given for making it easy to wear the stocking.
3
Hold the stocking using both hands and maintaining equal gap then stretch it and slide upward.
4
Stocking should be worn keeping the stitching outside and placing the vertical stitching at posterior central part of leg.
5
Keep the proximal end near to the gluteal fold and distal end at toes to avoid the chances of reverse gradient.
6
Wear thin socks over the stockings in feet specially covering the entire sole area.
7
Use elastic belt to tight the grip of stocking as it can hold the stocking.
8
Use waist belt and attach suspenders in stocking then adjust the height accordingly to prevent sliding.
9
If possible wear the waist belt above from the level of lower garment on waist.
10
Use the same method for donning and doffing to avoid the chances of skin allergy and garment damage.
